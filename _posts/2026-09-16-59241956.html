I attended the Society for Biotechnology, Japan meeting for the first time. I gave an invited talk at a symposium on transforming biotechnology through microbial research. A large audience came, and the talk went over reasonably well, which made me happy.

The symposium brought together not only our Transformative Research Areas (B) project but also other (B) projects, so I expect new connections to come out of it.
