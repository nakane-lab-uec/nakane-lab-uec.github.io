I introduced UEC and gave a lecture titled "Let's look at microbes with a smartphone." The high school students seemed to enjoy it. The school has a really beautiful campus near Fujinomiya in Shizuoka Prefecture. I hear some students from Seiryo High School have gone on to UEC, so I may meet them in class someday.
