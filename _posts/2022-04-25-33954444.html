On NHK E-Tele's "Science ZERO" (Sunday, April 24, from 11:30 pm), in the special "Monkeys, Koalas, and Stinkbugs Too! The 'Super' Powers of Animal Gut Bacteria", videos of bacterial swimming that I filmed with an optical microscope were shown with credit. They are high-precision measurements of "drill-tank" swimming, in which bacteria wrap their flagella around their bodies as they move. Kikuchi and his bean bugs got plenty of screen time. There is a rerun next Saturday.

---

４月24日（日）午後11時30分～NHK Eテレ サイエンスZERO 「サルもコアラもカメムシも！ アニマル腸内細菌の‟超"能力SP」で、細菌の遊泳運動を光学顕微鏡で撮影した動画がクレジットつきで紹介されました。べん毛を体に巻き付けて動く、ドリル戦車遊泳の様子を高精度に計測した動画です。菊池さん＆ホソヘリカメムシはがっつり登場していました。次の土曜日に再放送もあります。
