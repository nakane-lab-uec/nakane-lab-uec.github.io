The Young Researchers' Meeting of the Society of Genome Microbiology was supposed to be held in Atami this year, but unfortunately it moved online because of a typhoon. Sato was the only one from our lab to attend and present. He seems to be getting more comfortable with oral presentations.

---

ゲノム微生物学会若手の会は、今年は熱海で開催の予定でしたが、台風の影響で残念ながらオンライン開催となりました。うちのラボからは佐藤くんが1人の参加・発表です。口頭発表も少しずつ慣れてきたようです。
