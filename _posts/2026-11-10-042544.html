Daisuke gave an invited talk at the ATF Conference held in Nagano, Japan. The conference brought together researchers from a wide range of disciplines, offering a valuable opportunity for interdisciplinary exchange. In particular, the discussions on recent advances in AI were truly inspiring and provided fresh perspectives on the future of scientific research.

---

長野で開催されたATFカンファレンスにて、中根が招待講演を行いました。いろいろな分野との合同開催で、特に最近のAIの動向について、研究者の視点で大いに刺激を受けました。
