Lab assignment for undergraduate research has started at UEC. If you're wondering what the Nakane Lab is like, please come visit. You can talk with Nakane and the students in the lab, and watch experiments in progress. Please come to Room 413 in West Building 2.

I will be on campus on 11/18, 11/20, 11/21, 11/22, 11/24, 11/28, and 11/29. On 11/20 (Sun), during open campus, the lab is open from 10:00 to 17:00, so drop by whenever you like. If you want to come at another time, it helps to contact Nakane in advance. My email address is dice-k@uec.ac.jp.
