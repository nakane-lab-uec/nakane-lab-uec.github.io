Nakane gave an invited talk at the 14th Molecular Motors Symposium, held on September 11-12, 2025. It was my first time attending in 10 years. There was plenty of time for discussion, and I really enjoyed it. I took a stroll around Kyoto University for the first time in a while. Have the famous standing signboards disappeared at some point?
