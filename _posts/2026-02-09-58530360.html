The master's thesis presentations were on 2/5 and the bachelor's thesis presentations on 2/9. Yokohama, Narita, Kikkawa, Oinuma, and Ogura are completing their master's degrees, and Aihara and Ishibachi are graduating from the undergraduate program.

To celebrate a job well done, they made takoyaki.

おつかれさま、ということでたこ焼きをつくってくれました。
