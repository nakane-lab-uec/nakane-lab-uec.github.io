This year's meeting was held in Kochi. The venue was close to the downtown area and Kochi Castle, which made it easy to get to know people. Many students presented for the first time, and I think it was a good experience.

The last day was sunny, so everyone looks a little dazzled in the photo.

Fujimura and Ishibachi presented on Monday, and Kishikawa, Utsumi, and Sato on Tuesday.

---

今年は高知での開催でした。繁華街や高知城にも近くで、交流も深まりました。初めて発表した学生も多く、良い経験になったと思います。

最終日は晴天で、まぶしそうな写真になりました。

藤村くんと石鉢くんは月曜日、岸川くんと内海さんと佐藤くんは火曜日に発表でした。
