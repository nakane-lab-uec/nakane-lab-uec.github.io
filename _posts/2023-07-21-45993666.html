Second-year master's student Uemura attended and presented at the "Gordon Research Conference (Microbial Adhesion and Signal Transduction)," held in Newport, USA, from Sunday, July 16 to Friday, July 21, 2023.

It was his first international conference, and without his supervisor there, so I was a bit worried, but it looks like he had a great time. This is joint research with Prof. Tamakoshi.

Not only Uemura, but also "that T-shirt" made its international conference debut.


---

修士２年の上村くんが2023年7月16日（日）から7月21日（金）にアメリカのニューポートで開催された「Gordon Research Conference (Microbial Adhesion and Signal Transduction)」で参加・発表しました。

初めての国際学会＆指導教員不在で心配していましたが、エンジョイできたようです。玉腰先生との共同研究です。

上村くんだけでなく、例のTシャツも国際学会デビューですね。
