Two students from Chofu-kita High School have been coming to the lab to do experiments. They will present the results of their hard work over the summer at the Japanese Society of Microbial Ecology meeting. Their poster is printed at A0 size, and they are all set.

The title of their presentation is "Gourmet Microbes: What Do Microbes Eat?"

---

発表のタイトルは「グルメな微生物たち ～微生物は何を食べているの？～」です。
