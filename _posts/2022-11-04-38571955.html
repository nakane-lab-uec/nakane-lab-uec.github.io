First-year master's students Uemura and Yoshioka won Student Presentation Awards at the 35th Annual Meeting of the Japanese Society of Microbial Ecology. A double win, and they both look happy. Here they are in a commemorative photo with their co-author, Kikuchi from AIST.

Thank you to everyone who evaluated their work. Now we want to get it into a paper soon.

It was also featured on the UEC website.

---

修士１年の上村くん、吉岡くんが微生物生態学会 第35回大会にて、学生発表賞を受賞しました。ダブル受賞で２人とも嬉しそうです。共著者である産総研の菊池さんと一緒に記念撮影です。

受賞に関して、評価してくださった皆様、ありがとうございます。はやく論文にしたいところです。
