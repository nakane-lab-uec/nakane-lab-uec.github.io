Narita came to visit the lab. Great to see him doing well.

His boss from the company he now works for also came and explained what they do. Their presentation using the company's products was very easy to follow, and I think the students who are job hunting right now found it interesting too.

It's nice to hear about a company from a senior you know.

身近な先輩から会社の話を聞けるのもいいですよね。
