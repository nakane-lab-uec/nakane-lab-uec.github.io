This is the result of a collaboration between Kotaka of NIID and Tokyo Metropolitan University and our own Uemura. Kotaka visited the lab about a year and a half ago, and that's how the collaboration began. Apparently the two had shared a room at the E. coli research meeting. It's really wonderful that a chance encounter led to such a great result. The university's news release is here. The paper itself is here.
