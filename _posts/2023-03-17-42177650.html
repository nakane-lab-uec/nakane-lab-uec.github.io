First-year master's student Uemura received an Excellent Presentation Award at the 96th Annual Meeting of the Japanese Society for Bacteriology. The award is open to graduate students and those within one year of receiving their degree, and 15 presentations were selected out of about 150. Thank you to everyone who evaluated his work. The figures are gradually coming together, so a paper should not be far off. It was also featured on the UEC website.

---

修士1年の上村くんが第96回日本細菌学会総会にて、優秀発表賞を受賞しました。大学院生および学位取得1年未満のかたが対象で、約150演題の中から15演題が選ばれました。受賞に関して、評価してくださった皆様、ありがとうございます。図も少しずつできてきたので、論文にできる日も近いです。電通大のHPにも掲載されました。
