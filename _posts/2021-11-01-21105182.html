This was Yoshioka's conference debut. As one of the first students in the Nakane Lab, Yoshioka is really enjoying research. The presentation went well, and the handmade T-shirt was great too.

This year's Japanese Society of Microbial Ecology meeting was supposed to be held in Niigata, but it went online. I wish I could have had a drink in Niigata.
