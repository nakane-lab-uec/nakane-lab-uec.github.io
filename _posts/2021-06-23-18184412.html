It's about the huge vortex patterns made by soil bacteria. The movie is here.

The paper is here. The press release is here. It's also on the UEC website.

A related figure was also chosen for the cover of the next issue (coming soon).

We found that the bacterium Flavobacterium forms a huge vortex pattern when the cells gather in a group. For some reason, the vortex always turns counterclockwise. These are soil bacteria, so if you peeked into the soil, you might find them spinning round and round without anyone noticing. Odaka, a student from my time at Gakushuin University, did most of the hard work on the experiments.
