Uemura attended the HOPE meeting . I attended it too as a student, but I happened to come down with a rare cold and spent the time in bed, so I don't have many memories of it. About 15 years later, my own student got the redo. Looks like he had a great time. He even asked a Nobel laureate a question in English.
