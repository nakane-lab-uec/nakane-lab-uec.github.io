The paper is here. The press release is here. It's also on the UEC website.

We found that a small bacterium has a molecule that works as a "ruler." By artificially modifying the ruler molecule to make it shorter/longer, we can make the attachment organelle, a supramolecular structure about 300 nm long, shorter/longer. Murata, a student from my time at Gakushuin University, and Kenri at the National Institute of Infectious Diseases made lots of mutant strains and ran the experiments. Many thanks! Murata has received Excellent Presentation Awards at the Annual Meeting of the Japanese Society for Bacteriology and the Annual Meeting of the Japanese Society of Mycoplasmology.

小さな細菌には「ものさし」として機能する分子があることを見つけました。ものさし分子を人為的に改変して 短く/長く すると、接着器官という 300 nm ほどの超分子構造体を 短く/長く 変化させることが可能です。学習院大学所属時の学生 村田くんと感染研の見理さんがたくさん変異株をつくって実験をしてくれました。感謝です。村田くんは日本細菌学会総会や日本マイコプラズマ学会学術集会で優秀発表賞を受賞しています。
