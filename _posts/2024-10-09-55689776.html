I gave a visiting lecture at Tokyo Metropolitan Komatsugawa High School, talking for about two hours to first- and second-year students. Afterwards I received lots of comments, which made me really happy.

You can get there from UEC on a single train, from Chofu Station to Higashi-ojima Station with no transfers.
