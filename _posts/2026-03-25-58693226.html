From the Nakane Lab, master's students Yokohama, Kikkawa, and Narita have left the lab. Ogura and Oinuma, who were officially affiliated with Tokyo University of Agriculture and Technology and the Shintake Lab but did their experiments in the Nakane Lab, also completed their master's degrees. We wish them all the best in their new places.

Fourth-year students Aihara and Ishibachi are graduating and moving on to graduate school. That means about two more years of research together.

Here is a photo of the graduation ceremony sign. I took it early in the morning, so there is no line. If you edit yourself into it, you can make a commemorative photo without waiting in line.

中根研からは、修士の横濱さん、橘川くん、成田くんがラボを旅立ちました。また、農工大や新竹研が本所属ながら中根研で実験してくれた小倉くんや生沼さんも修士で卒業しました。皆さん、新天地で活躍されることを願っています。

4年生の相原くんと石鉢くんは卒業したのち、大学院に進学です。あと2年ほど研究できますね。
