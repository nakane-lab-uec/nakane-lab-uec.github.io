We held a Japan–Taiwan bilateral symposium at the Taiwan biophysical society meeting. Our Taiwanese hosts took wonderful care of us. Thank you!

The meeting was held in Hsinchu, Taiwan. As a souvenir for the lab members, I bought Hsinchu pure rice noodles. They were delicious rice vermicelli.
