I went to the US for the first time in a long while (six years?). It was my first trip outside Asia since before COVID. The meeting was Sensory Transduction in Microorganisms , which has about 50 years of history.

Photos aren't allowed at the conference, so here is a photo of a nearby beach instead. It was in Ventura, California, and nice and warm.
