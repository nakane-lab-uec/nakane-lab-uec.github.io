Nakane's lecture has been posted on a site called Yume Navi. It's a site for high school students where they can watch lectures by faculty from many universities. It looks like an interesting initiative. Registration apparently has to be done by a high school or grade as a whole.

I'm not used to being filmed, so I'm a little nervous. If I get questions from high school students, I'll do my best to answer them.
