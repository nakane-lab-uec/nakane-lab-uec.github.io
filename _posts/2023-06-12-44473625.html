Our research area "Why Microbes Move" is hosting the "Seeing Microbes Summer School," 8/22-24 at The University of Electro-Communications. Participation is free!

Why not join us and watch microbes move around?
If you would like to participate, please fill out the registration form. The deadline is Friday, 7/7.
