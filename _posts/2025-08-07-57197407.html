Bacteria living in hot springs move against the flow. When swimming upstream, they attach to the surface, stand up, and flap around as if dancing. Uemura worked hard on this research.

This work was published in The ISME Journal.
UEC also issued a news release.
EurekAlert! also featured it.

Here is a video of the rheotaxis.

温泉に住む細菌は流れに逆らって運動します。流れに逆らうときには、地面にくっついて立ち上がり、パタパタと踊るような振る舞いを見せます。上村くんが頑張って研究をしてくれました。
