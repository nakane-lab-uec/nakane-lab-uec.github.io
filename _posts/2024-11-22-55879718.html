Our lab will be open on Sunday, November 24, from 11:00 to 17:00. Please come to Room 413, West Building 2.

This is part of the UEC open campus. Everyone is welcome: junior high and high school students, UEC students, anyone. You can see for yourself what research we do, what equipment we have, and what daily life is like for our students. You can also try simple experiments with our undergraduate and graduate students, and even observe microbes with your own smartphone.


To UEC third-year students

It's almost time for lab assignments. If you're interested, feel free to drop by and visit. You can come during the open campus, or at the following times when I'll be in the lab:

Tue 11/26, afternoon
Wed 11/27, afternoon
Thu 11/28, all day
Fri 11/29, all day (except 4th period)
12/3-5 are also open.
