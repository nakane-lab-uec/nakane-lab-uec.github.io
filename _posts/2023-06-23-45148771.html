At the "Gordon Research Conference (Animal-Microbe Symbioses)," held in Italy from Sunday, June 18 to Friday, June 23, 2023, second-year master's student Yoshioka received a poster award.

It was his first international conference, and without his supervisor there, so I was a bit worried, but it looks like he had a great time. This is joint research with Kikuchi and Kan! Thank you to everyone who evaluated his work.

---

修士２年の吉岡くんが2023年6月18日（日）から6月23日（金）にイタリアで開催された「Gordon Research Conference (Animal-Microbe Symbioses)」にて、ポスター賞を受賞しました。

初めての国際学会＆指導教員不在で心配していましたが、エンジョイできたようです。菊池さん菅さんとの共同研究です！評価してくださったみなさま、ありがとうございます。
