Our joint work with Prof. Inoue and Ph.D. student Sugiyama at Osaka University was published in Environmental Microbiology . Bdellovibrio is a bacterium that eats other bacteria. The study compared two closely related species. Nakane and Uemura helped with motility observations and electron microscopy of the flagella. Congratulations!!

Bdellovibrio is famous for moving fast. There are videos too, so take a look.

阪大の井上先生や博士学生の杉山さんとの共著の成果が Environmental Microbiology 誌に掲載されました。デロビブリオという細菌を喰う細菌がいます。この近縁2種を比較しました。中根と上村くんが運動観察やべん毛の電子顕微鏡観察でお手伝いしました。おめでとうございます！！
