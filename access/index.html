From Chofu Station, the easiest way is to follow the red line shown below.
Enter through the South Gate, walk through the campus, turn left partway along, and go through the Middle Gate. There is a crosswalk in front of the Middle Gate; cross it to get from the East Campus to the West Campus. Then just head for West Building 2.
